# Atom — product positioning

## The promise

**Teach an AI employee your business. Give it work. Come back to a result.**

Atom is an open-source, locally run workspace for company data, taught methods
and durable business work. Lead with a useful job a visitor can reproduce:
a sample employee, a CSV, **232990.00**, and a downloadable document.

This repository is already public on GitHub. The open-source app should be
useful on its own; hosted operation is a separate concern. Do not invent a
SaaS URL, availability date, enterprise SLA or feature promise.

## Who it is for

- People who repeatedly prepare quotes, review structured records or produce
  documents from spreadsheets.
- Small teams and technical operators who want an inspectable local setup.
- Contributors exploring teaching, deterministic policies and durable execution.

The qualified local topology is single worker. Do not market the local
acceptance as a hosted multi-tenant or multi-worker production qualification.

## What to show

1. **First useful result:** setup → login → sample employee → 232990.00 → DOCX.
2. **Teach and correct:** contextual format/method, missing qualification,
   conflicting teaching, revised work adopting the correction.
3. **Unfamiliar data:** actual headers → user-confirmed mapping → cited result.
4. **Durable work:** wait → real restart → same assignment resumes with preserved
   completed work and no duplicate controlled effect.
5. **Policy execution:** ordered deterministic rules with unresolved inputs
   retained, distinct from discretionary model judgment.

Use the [demo guide](../demo/VERIFIED_WORKFLOWS.md). Historical campaigns are
source-bound evidence, not a claim that following a demo repeats every campaign.

## Evidence language

| Use | Do not turn it into |
|---|---|
| 111/123 frozen functional claims accepted in documented local/controlled configurations | 90% accuracy, universal reliability or every feature production-ready |
| About 5 min 15 s measured with warm caches on one host | Guaranteed setup time or a measured cold install |
| 15-minute cold-install target | A completed benchmark |
| Qualified deterministic taught policy | Qualified discretionary-model judgment |
| Controlled effect and duplicate prevention | Real-provider delivery |
| Separately accepted one-use Outlook send and Microsoft read-only ingestion | All connectors work or all autonomous live sending is qualified |
