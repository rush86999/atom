## Decision

- Handoff slice: **verified** on this candidate/world.
- Release candidate: **not deployable**. The launch promise still requires the
  calculation and every review subject to complete within the declared turn
  budget without planning failure. No deadline widening, no model swap to
  conceal latency, no retry-to-pass.
- Next bounded work (separate slices):
  1. planning robustness: the confirmation/review read must not depend on the
     optional planner choosing a capability; use the registered dataset reader
     directly when the accepted task already names the workbook and subjects,
     and never classify a workbook column name (e.g. `Notes`) as a required
     source kind;
  2. latency accounting: identify the pre-reply work in the derivation lane
     (planner started at 19.4s but the reply leg began at 90.3s) and make the
     turn clock bound the whole request, so a user gets either the answer or a
     prompt, truthful blocker — not a late timeout;
  3. re-run one isolated rehearsal from a pre-taught snapshot after those
     slices; attempt three remains untouched and no fourth cutover is
     authorized.
